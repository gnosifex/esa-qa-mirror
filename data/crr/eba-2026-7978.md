# EBA Q&A 2026_7978

## Question

In template F 46.00, for movements related to equity instruments designated at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI), the cell corresponding to “Retained earnings” has been opened, while the corresponding cell under “Other reserves” remains closed.

Could the EBA clarify whether the transfer of accumulated OCI upon derecognition of FVOCI equity instruments should be reported under “Retained earnings” in F 46.00? If not, should the corresponding cell under “Other reserves” be opened for this purpose?

## Background

Under IFRS 9, for investments in equity instruments designated at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI), amounts recognised in OCI are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss upon derecognition. However, the cumulative gain or loss may be transferred within equity.

In the current configuration of template F 46.00, the cell corresponding to “Retained earnings” has been opened for the relevant movement, while the corresponding cell under “Other reserves” remains closed.

This raises a question as to the intended FINREP treatment, since the transfer of the accumulated OCI balance upon derecognition of FVOCI equity instruments may be recognised as a transfer within reserves rather than as a movement in retained earnings.

## Answer

In template F 46.00, the transfer of accumulated other comprehensive income (OCI) upon derecognition of equity instruments designated at FVOCI should be reported in the cell corresponding to “Retained earnings”. This representation is consistent with the illustrative case presented in the Implementation Guidance in IFRS 7 (IG11B) and it aims at promoting a harmonised approach for reporting in template F 46, although institutions may use alternative representations in their public financial statements.
