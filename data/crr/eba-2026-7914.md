# EBA Q&A 2026_7914

## Question

For the purpose of reporting its “Accumulated equity interest [%]” and respectively the “Voting rights [%]” in respect of S2, should P use (a) P’s own indirect controlling interest of 48% (80% * 60%) or (b) S1’s direct controlling interest of 60%?

## Background

The parent entity P has a controlling interest of 80% in its subsidiary S1, which further has a controlling interest of 60% in its subsidiary S2. The mentioned percentages reflect both the related accumulated equity interest and the related voting rights.

## Answer

Assuming that the submitter is referring to the template F 40.01 included in Annex I to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/3117, the reporting of 'Accumulated equity interest %' and 'Voting rights % in this template refers to the relationship between the reporting institution and its investees.

In the example provided, if the reporting institution is the parent entity P, it will report its 'Accumulated equity interest %' and 'Voting rights %' towards the subsidiary S 1 and towards the subsidiary S2 (for the latter using the indirect method).

Regarding the reporting of 'Accumulated equity interest %' in template F 40.02, please refer to the Q&A 2024_7222 published on the EBA website.
