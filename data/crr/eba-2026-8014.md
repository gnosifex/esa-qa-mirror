# EBA Q&A 2026_8014

## Question

F22 prescribes reporting of Assets under Custody, where the full amount of such assets is reported in F22.02, r0060c0010, and fees and commission income on such assets are reported in F22.01, r0080c0010. Regulation 2024/3117, Article 22, Annex V, paragraph 21, specifies that only Financial Instruments measured at Fair Value through Profit or Loss should be included. At the same time, the regulation does not clarify how Assets under Depositary (i.e. collective investment undertakings subject to both AIFMD and UCITS) should be treated. Should all Assets under Depositary be included in the F22 reporting, or should the scope depend on the underlying depositary assets (e.g. transferable securities be included, while other assets such as cash, loans, real estate, should be excluded)? Could you please also clarify if only Assets under Custody/Depositary services that are provided by the reporting institution itself should be in the scope of F22 reporting, or assets entrusted to sub-custodians should be reported as well?

## Background

As a credit institution, we provide Custody, as well as Depositary services to our customers (either directly, or via sub-custodians). Annex V of FINREP reporting does not explicitly describe Depositary reporting requirements. This question aims to clarify expectations in regards Assets under Depositary reporting.

## Answer

The IT solutions (Annex V, Part 2, paragraph 281) to Regulation (EU) 2024/3117 state the following:

‘The fee and commission income and expenses shall be reported by type of activity. Under IFRS, this template shall include fee and commission income and expenses other than both of the following:

(a) amounts considered for the calculation of the effective interest of financial instruments (IFRS 7.20.(c));

(b) amounts arising from financial instruments that are measured at fair value through profit or loss (IFRS 7.20.(c)(i))’.

In other terms, fee and commission income and expenses arising from financial assets and financial liabilities that are not at fair value through profit or loss shall be reported in templates F 22, unless their amounts are included in determining the effective interest rate.

The IT solutions (Annex V, Part 2, paragraph 285, letter b) to Regulation (EU) 2024/3117 state the following:

'‘Custody assets’ shall refer to the services of safekeeping and administration of financial instruments for the account of clients provided by the institution and services related to custodianship such as cash and collateral management. ‘Custody assets’ shall be reported by type of customers for which the institution is holding the assets distinguishing between collective investment undertakings and others. The item ‘of which: entrusted to other entities’ shall refer to the amount of assets included in custody assets for which the institution has given the effective custody to other entities’.

Considering the instructions mentioned above and the provisions of IFRS 7.20(c), pursuant to which fee and commission income and expenses arising from fiduciary activities involving the holding or investing of assets on behalf of third parties are to be reported, and considering that both custody and depositary services constitute such fiduciary activities, the custody services provided by the reporting institution itself or entrusted to sub-custodians, regardless of the underlying asset types, should be included in the templates F22. For further guidance on the assets to be reported in template F 22, please also refer to Q&A 2017_3334.
