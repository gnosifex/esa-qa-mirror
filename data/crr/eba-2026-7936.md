# EBA Q&A 2026_7936

## Question

Under Annex V to the ITS on Supervisory Reporting, template F 16.08 requires institutions to report other administrative expenses including "IT outsourcing".

Following the application of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), institutions may incur expenses and losses related to ICT third-party service providers.

Should such expenses and losses be reported in FINREP template F 16.08 under the row "IT outsourcing" whenever they are attributable to outsourced ICT services, including ICT services falling within the scope of DORA?

Furthermore, should the reporting treatment differ depending on whether the loss event originates from:

(a) the institution's own ICT systems,

or

(b) an ICT third-party service provider?

## Background

The introduction of DORA has increased the use of a specific regulatory framework for ICT third-party risk management. However, the FINREP instructions do not explicitly clarify whether DORA-related ICT outsourcing incidents should be reported under the "IT outsourcing" category or another operational risk category.

## Answer

Under the Commission Implementing Regulation EU 2025/302 (ITS on incident reporting), financial entities are required to report the gross direct and indirect costs and losses resulting from a major ICT-related incident, as set out in Annexes I and II of these ITS on incident reporting, data field n. 4.13.

In FINREP, these costs and losses should be reported in the appropriate items of the Income statement pursuant to their nature and to the classification used in the institution's public financial statements. Therefore, for example, expenses paid to ICT third-party providers should be reported in 'Other administrative expenses- IT outsourcing' of template F 16.08. For other expenses and losses, the reporting will depend on their specific nature (e.g. impairment on software assets, provisions, other operating expenses) and it should be consistent with the classification used for accounting purposes in the public financial statements.
