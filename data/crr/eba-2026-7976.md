# EBA Q&A 2026_7976

## Question

If an institution does business with a branch abroad, how does the institution report information regarding geographical breakdown by residence of the counterparty in table F 20.04 – taking into account the country of the branches' residence or the country of the head office residence? For example, Swedish Institution A does business with Branch B in Sweden, which is part of Institution C in Denmark. Should Institution A report in template F 20.04 their exposure to Branch B of Institution C as an exposure to Denmark (residence of head office) or as an exposure to Sweden (residence of branch)?

## Background

Annex V, Part 2.271: Templates F 20.04 to F 20.07 contain information "country-by-country" on the basis of the residence of the immediate counterparty as defined in paragraph 43 of Part 1 of this Annex. The breakdown provided shall include exposures or liabilities with residents in each foreign country in which the institution has exposures.

## Answer

Pursuant to the IT solutions set out in Annex V, Part 2.271 of Regulation (EU) 2024/3117, templates F 20.4 to F 20.7 require institutions to report information on a country‑by‑country basis using the residence of the immediate counterparty, as defined in paragraph 43 of Part 1 of Annex V. The breakdown must therefore reflect exposures or liabilities to residents in each foreign country where the institution has exposures.

Because a branch is not considered a separate legal entity from its head office, the residence of the immediate counterparty corresponds to the residence of the legal entity itself. In the example provided by the submitter, institution A should therefore report its exposure to branch B of institution C in template F 20.04 as an exposure to Denmark where the residence of institution C is situated.

A different treatment is provided for templates F 20.1 to F 20.3 where the breakdown is by location of the activities (Annex V, Part 2.270). This means the jurisdiction of the residence of the branch, that is Sweden (reported as a domestic activity) in the example provided.
