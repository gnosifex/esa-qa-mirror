# EBA Q&A 2025_7617

## Question

Should accrued interest on withdrawable central bank reserves be reported on Row 050 and Row 100 of C72.00 template?

## Background

The LCR Delegated Regulation Article 10(1)(b) and Article 10(1)(d) defines withdrawable central bank reserves as Level 1 High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA). However, the treatment of accrued interest that is withdrawable along with these reserves is not explicitly defined.

A previous EBA Q&A 2013_154 clarified that market value of a liquid asset is its ‘dirty price’. However, this guidance specifically refers to debt securities and does not address central bank reserves.

## Answer

Provided that the accrued interest on central bank reserves are withdrawable within the meaning of Articles 10(1)(b)(iii) and 10(1)(d)(ii) of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/61, they shall be treated and reported accordingly.

For the purpose of the above, credit institutions should consider the remuneration policies and accrual methods specified in the relevant central bank frameworks.
